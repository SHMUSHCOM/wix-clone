# Home assignment for product managers

## Overview

- This task is part of the WixStores product. Please familiarize yourself with the WixStores solution before starting. The solution should fit within the existing Wix offering.
- The goal is to test your ability to understand the business logic of a product (or feature) and translate it into a product through wireframes.
- The task is a "home task".
- After your submission is reviewed, we will inform you on how your recruitment process will continue.
- We encourage you to use AI based tools responsibly. While there are great tools out there, deliverables are not good enough without applying your product sense. Make sure you can explain and stand behind what you submit.

## Task

The task is to add a Buyback (AKA re-commerce) option to WixStores.

This capability will allow customers to sell their used items back to a Wix store: a store that sells a product offers customers the option to sell back their used product and receive compensation for it, in some form.

## Scope

1. **KPIs:** Define main Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) for this feature.
2. **Business Manager (Merchant Side):** Design the flows for the store owners to configure and manage the buyback process.
3. **Storefront (Buyer Side):** Design the flows for customers. There is no need to demonstrate how the design of the interface is created (Editor).
4. The process of re-selling the received product is not needed and out of scope.

## Requirements

- Create wireframes only (avoid textual documents as those are not required). You may include notes on the wireframes, but the product should be outlined and self-explanatory through the wireframes.
- Use any method you prefer for creating wireframes, such as Balsamiq, Figma, etc.
- Provide wireframes for desktop screens only. Mobile views are not required.

## Submission

Please make sure your submission includes:

- Wireframes for both the backend manager and storefront.
- You can use any method you like to draw the wireframes, such as Axure, Balsamiq, PowerPoint or any other method you are used to. Please send the final wireframes in a PDF or Google slides format.
- Main KPIs.
